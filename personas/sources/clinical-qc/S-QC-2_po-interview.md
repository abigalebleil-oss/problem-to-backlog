# S-QC-2 · Interview with a clinical lab QC software product owner (fictional)

Type: interview. Synthetic notes for the sandbox. Interviewee: "M. Chen", PO, QC software, 6 years (fictional).

## Who uses it
- "Bench techs run controls and react to flags."
- "The QC coordinator or quality manager reviews everything."
- "The lab director signs the QC policy."
- "Inspectors read our records."

## What customers judge us on (ranked)
1. "Catching QC failures before a bad result reaches a clinician."
2. "Not crying wolf. False rejections burn reruns and trust."
3. "Every failure has documented corrective action."
4. "Records that are inspection-ready."

## Never
- "Patient results after a QC reject don't report until QC is acceptable and corrective action is documented."
- "QC rule changes are attributable, dated and director-approved."
- "A QC event, its corrective action and the patient results it touched stay linked."

## Every requirement must say
- "Which QC decision it changes, if any."
- "How it's verified."

## Words that cause trouble
- "Accurate and reliable — say which metric. And the usual vague ones."

## What I ask first
- "Which rules and frequencies does lab policy set?"
- "What corrective action must be documented before release?"
- "How do we find patient results run since the last good QC?"

## Who signs off
- "Me, a lab quality SME, the software lead, and QA/RA."
