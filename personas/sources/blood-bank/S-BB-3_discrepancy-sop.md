# S-BB-3 · Serologic discrepancy resolution SOP, section 3 (fictional customer site)

Type: SOP excerpt from a fictional customer site, shared with permission for product design.

3.1 ABO/Rh discrepancies are resolved by a qualified technologist only. Instrument or software
    automation may flag but must not resolve a discrepancy.
3.2 Every resolution is reviewed by a second qualified technologist or supervisor before release.
    The record states who resolved, who reviewed, the time of each step and the reason.
3.3 If the sample label does not match the request, the result is not reported and a new sample is collected.
